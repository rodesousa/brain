You're paying for Claude and getting 10% of it.

Right now someone is using the exact same $20/month subscription you have. Same model. Same features. And they're building a content operation that runs while they sleep, replacing tools that cost them $3,000/month, and making decisions faster than anyone on their team.

The difference isn't that they're smarter. It's that they set up 12 things you probably skipped because nobody told you they existed.

## \[ THE FOUNDATION \]

1\. Build your CLAUDE.md before anything else

Claude knows nothing about you by default. Every conversation starts from zero. CLAUDE.md is a document you create once - your tone, your rules, your audience, what you never want to see. Without it, Claude guesses. With it, output sounds like you wrote it.

PROMPT ↓

```text
Interview me with 10 questions to build my CLAUDE.md. 

Cover: who I am, my exact audience, my tone in 3 words, my hard rules, my goals this month, and 3 examples of my best work. 

One question at a time. After I answer all 10 - write the final file in under 200 lines.
```

2\. Use Projects, not chats

One-off conversations are for tourists. Projects are where Claude compounds. Your CLAUDE.md lives there. Your files live there. Claude gets smarter every week because context builds. Week 1 and week 8 inside the same Project are completely different tools.

3\. Show Claude what good looks like before you ask

Paste your 5 best pieces of work and say "this is the standard." Every output after that has a real target. Without this step Claude is guessing what good means in your context.

4\. Feed real data, not descriptions

"My business is doing okay" tells Claude nothing. Paste actual numbers, actual metrics, actual situation. Vague in = vague out. Every time.

## \[ THE WORKFLOW \]

5\. One Project per goal, not one for everything

One Project for content. One for client work. One for research. Mixing everything kills the context that makes Claude useful.

6\. Audit your Project every two weeks

PROMPT ↓

```text
Review my Project instructions and files. 

Tell me: what context is missing that would make you more accurate. 

What you're currently guessing that I should just tell you directly

What files would help most if I uploaded them. I want gaps, not compliments.
```

7\. Never use Claude to confirm what you already think

You ask a leading question. Claude validates you. You feel smart. Nothing changes. Claude only adds value if you let it tell you when you're wrong.

8\. Stress-test every important decision

PROMPT ↓

```text
Here is the decision I'm about to make: [describe it]

1. What has to be true for this to work
2. Which assumption is weakest
3. Who is on the other side of this and what do they know that I don't
4. What would make you tell me not to do this

Don't validate me. Stress-test me.
```

## \[ THE LEVERAGE \]

9\. Set up at least one Scheduled Task

Claude runs tasks on a schedule without you. Daily briefing. Weekly competitor summary. Monthly audit. One evening of setup - runs forever.

10\. Build Skills for anything you repeat more than 3 times

If you're explaining the same thing to Claude more than 3 times - it should be a Skill. Build it once, runs exactly the same every time, you never explain it again.

11\. Use the 1M token context for what it was built for

Opus 4.6 has the largest context window of any frontier model. Stop using it for single questions. Feed it an entire codebase. An entire year of emails. An entire content archive. Then ask it to find patterns nobody else saw.

12\. Update your CLAUDE.md every month

Your business changes. Your priorities change. Claude doesn't know unless you tell it. Treat CLAUDE.md like a living document, not a one-time setup.

## Additional

Most people will read this, recognize themselves in steps 1 through 4, and do nothing.

Some will spend this weekend building the foundation.

The gap between those two groups is already visible in what they're producing.
